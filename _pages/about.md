Hi there! I'm Timo, a PhD student in Linguistics at [UC Davis](https://linguistics.ucdavis.edu/people/timothy-liu). I graduated with highest honors and a BS in Computational Cognitive Science from UC Davis in 2024, alongside a minor in Computer Science. My non-academic interests include Archery, Game Development, and 2D Art. I have acquired [prosopagnosia](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/23412-prosopagnosia-face-blindness), so if I don't immediately recognize you, it's probably not because I don't remember you; I probably just can't tell who you are.

# Research
My interests swirl around the intersection of natural language processing, psycholinguistics, and computational linguistics. My honors thesis investigated the role of orthography in online lexical access, and I am currently experimenting with phonologically informed tokenization paradigms for Large Language Models. I work with David Corina and Kenji Sagae.
