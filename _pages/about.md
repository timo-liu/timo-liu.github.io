Hi! I'm Timo (thy). I'm a PhD student in the Linguistics program at UC Davis. My current interests are in subword processing in Large Language Models (tokenization) and in humans (syllables and phonemes).

Following a stroke in 2019, I have acquired [prosopagnosia](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/23412-prosopagnosia-face-blindness), so if I don't say hi, it's just because I don't recognize you, not because I don't recognize you.
